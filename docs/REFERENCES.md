# References

Entries are grouped by use and cited in the text as (Author Year). Letters separate entries by the same authors in the
same year. Every paper DOI was checked against its Crossref record (title, authors, year, volume, pages) and the DOIs of
the CIE data sets against the CIE site. Where no DOI exists the entry names the archive record that was read instead.
